1. Introduction

Cheesy Dreams is a school business dedicated to making and selling delicious cheesecakes in two flavors: lemon and passion fruit. Our product aims to offer a sweet, attractive, and high-quality option for students, teachers, and visitors at the school’s business fair.


This idea was created to offer a delicious product while learning about entrepreneurship, organization, customer service, costs, and teamwork through a practical experience.

2. Mission & 3. Vision

💗

Mission

Our mission is to offer delicious, fresh, and well-presented cheesecakes made with dedication and responsibility. We aim to provide our customers with a pleasant experience and a product they will truly enjoy.

🌟

Vision

By 2027, we aim for Cheesy Dreams to be recognized within our school community as a creative and responsible business, standing out for the quality, flavor, and presentation of our cheesecakes.
